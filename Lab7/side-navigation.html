Clownfish

Clownfish are small, colorful fish commonly found in coral reefs. They live among sea anemones and are known for their bright orange and white stripes.

Goldfish

Goldfish are popular freshwater fish often kept as pets. They are easy to care for and come in various colors and sizes.

Shark

Sharks are large marine fish known as top predators in the ocean. They have sharp teeth and excellent senses for hunting prey.

Tuna

Tuna are fast-swimming ocean fish commonly used in food industries. They migrate long distances and live in deep open waters.
